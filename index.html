FROM TEMPLATE TO POLYMER

A little chemistry.
A lot of possibility.

  1. Choose your headgroup

    Choose primary ammonium, quaternary ammonium, guanidinium, or imidazolium. Each repeat unit carries one positive formal charge. The R in each headgroup image marks its connection to the side chain.

  2. Set the alkyl chain length

    Choose 4, 6, 8, or 10 total carbons after the ester oxygen. A C4 chain contains three CH₂ groups and one terminal CH₃. The selected monomer reference remains visible above the polymer.

  3. Choose a repeat count and build

    Choose 1–30 repeat units. The builder connects C4 of each unit to C1 of the next. One capping hydrogen is removed from each connection endpoint; the C5 methyl substituent stays in place. Each outer terminal hydrogen is replaced by a new CH₃ group, giving a methyl cap at both ends.

  4. Explore and export

    Inspect the original H-capped monomer reference alongside the CH₃-capped polymer. Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom. PDB and SDF include both terminal methyl groups and their hydrogens. The JSON recipe records your settings, end caps, and source provenance.

What the model represents

An initial, unminimized single polymer chain assembled from the supplied templates. The model contains no counterions or solvent. Tacticity is not controlled. Source monomer force-field parameters are not exported as a ready-to-run polymer topology.
